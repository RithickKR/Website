Harnessing the power of next-generation drone technology, our LiDAR and thermal inspection services provide unparalleled insight into your critical infrastructure. Equipped with high-resolution thermal cameras and precision LiDAR sensors, our UAV systems detect hidden faults, structural anomalies, and energy inefficiencies, delivering a multi-dimensional view of industrial assets and building envelopes.

  • Electrical system diagnostics with pinpoint hotspot detection

Industrial Facility Preventive Maintenance

Thermal and LiDAR inspection on an electrical distribution system at a manufacturing plant exposed critical overheating components. Early detection allowed for timely maintenance, preventing costly failures and unscheduled shutdowns.

  • Scope: Electrical distribution and mechanical systems
  • Findings: Overheating fault detected and mitigated
  • Improvement: Improved maintenance scheduling and equipment lifespan
  • What types of anomalies can thermal imaging detect?

    Thermal imaging highlights temperature variations caused by electrical faults (like arcing or loose connections), moisture intrusion, insulation deficiencies, mechanical wear, and energy leaks often invisible to standard visual inspections.

  • How accurate are your thermal inspections?

    Our high-end thermal sensors can detect differences as small as 0.05°C, allowing precise identification of minor issues before they escalate into costly failures.

  • What advantages does LiDAR add to inspections?

    LiDAR provides detailed 3D spatial information, enabling exact measurements, volume calculations, and structural analysis. It complements thermal data by mapping asset geometries and surroundings in precision.

  • What deliverables do you provide?

    We deliver comprehensive, easy-to-understand reports which combine annotated thermal imagery, visual photos, LiDAR point clouds, and actionable recommendations in digital (PDF, CAD, GIS) formats.

  • Are drone-based thermal inspections safe?

    Yes, drones safely inspect high-risk or hard-to-reach areas without exposing personnel to danger. Our pilots operate fully compliant with regulatory requirements ensuring safe and efficient flights.
