Enter the Impossible: Flyability Elios 3

When human entry is too dangerous, too costly, or simply impossible—BRADS Aerospace deploys the Flyability Elios 3, the world's most advanced collision-tolerant drone designed specifically for confined and GPS-denied environments.

Why Elios 3 is a Game-Changer

The Elios 3 isn't just a drone—it's a complete inspection ecosystem built to navigate where others can't. From boilers and tanks to silos, sewers, and underground mines, this robust platform eliminates the need to send personnel into hazardous spaces.

Technical Specifications

  • 48cm
    Width (Collision Cage)
  • 2.35kg
    Weight with LiDAR
  • 12.5min
    Max Flight Time
  • IP68
    LiDAR Protection Rating

Core Capabilities

  • ✅ Collision-Resilient Carbon Fiber Cage: Built to withstand multiple impacts without damage
  • ✅ FlyAware™ SLAM Engine: Real-time 3D localization powered by Nvidia Xavier NX
  • ✅ High-Performance LiDAR (Ouster OS0-32): Create accurate 3D point clouds and digital twins
  • ✅ 4K UHD Camera + Thermal Imaging: Crystal-clear close-ups plus heat anomaly detection
  • ✅ 16,000-Lumen Lighting System: Adaptive controls for total darkness environments
  • ✅ Modular Payload Bay: Supports ultrasonic testing, gas detection, radiation surveying
  • ✅ Smart Return-to-Signal: Auto-retrace path if signal is lost

Applications Across Industries

Inspect flare stacks, pressure vessels, storage tanks, and pipeline interiors with zero downtime. Detect corrosion, cracks, and weld defects while operations continue.

Boiler tubes, turbine casings, cooling towers, and penstock interiors—all inspected without scaffolding or rope access teams. Thermal imaging identifies hot spots before they become failures.

Explore ore passes, stopes, and underground voids safely. Generate volumetric data for resource estimation and structural analysis without blasting or drilling.

Cement kilns, furnaces, reactors, and storage silos—the Elios 3 maps internal geometries and identifies build-up, blockages, or structural anomalies instantly.

Data That Works for You

Every Elios 3 flight delivers actionable intelligence through Inspector 4.0 software:

  • Full HD live video transmission with UAV telemetry
  • Automated defect detection and geo-tagging
  • Instant reporting for regulatory compliance
  • Integration with existing asset management systems
  • Multi-user collaboration tools for remote teams

"The Elios 3 has fundamentally transformed how we approach confined space safety in India. What used to take days of preparation and put lives at risk now happens in minutes—with better data and zero human exposure."

— Abilash S, Head of Operations, BRADS Aerospace

Industry-Leading Safety Standards

By eliminating the need for human entry into hazardous confined spaces, the Elios 3 dramatically reduces risks associated with:

  • Toxic gas exposure
  • Oxygen deficiency
  • Structural instability
  • Heat stress
  • Poor visibility and lighting
  • Entrapment hazards
