Monitoring Services – Proactive Asset Surveillance & Condition Tracking

Accurate, continuous monitoring is critical to preventing costly failures and enhancing the lifecycle of industrial assets. Our drone-enabled solutions provide scalable, high-frequency surveillance with minimal disruption, ensuring real-time insights and superior operational readiness.

What We Provide

  • Continuous Structural Health Monitoring: Using repeat flyovers, we track corrosion progression, surface deformation, and structural fatigue before they turn critical.
  • Thermal Performance Monitoring: Periodic thermal scans detect leaks, electrical faults, and insulation degradation in pipelines, transformers, and industrial assets.
  • Encroachment & Vegetation Tracking: Automated mapping of vegetation growth and unauthorized activities on pipeline ROWs, storage sites, and utility corridors helps maintain safety and compliance.
  • Environmental & Spill Detection: Frequent aerial assessments identify soil contamination or chemical spills early to enable rapid remediation.
  • Construction Progress & Compliance: Regular 3D mapping and site capture support accurate progress tracking and audit readiness.
  • Weather & Disaster Response: Post-storm drone assessments rapidly evaluate damage at power grids, telecom towers, and industrial sites to prioritize repairs.

Key Features

  • RTK-enabled autonomous & manual flight modes for centimeter-level repeatability
  • Multi-sensor integration (HD visual, thermal, LiDAR) for rich data capture
  • Advanced change detection & automated analytics flagging anomalies
  • Geo-tagged, timestamped reports & dashboards for asset management systems
  • Scalable from single assets to wide-area industrial sites with flexible scheduling
  • Full regulatory & DGCA compliance across industries

Ideal For

Oil & gas pipelines, petrochemical plants, ports, stadiums, telecom towers, renewable energy farms, construction sites, and utility infrastructure.

Monitoring Workflow

Planning & Risk Assessment

Define monitoring objectives for industrial, environmental, or crowd settings. Assess site risks, environmental constraints, airspace regulations, and stakeholder requirements.

Customized Sensor Deployment

Deploy drones equipped with appropriate sensors — multispectral, thermal, LiDAR, or HD cameras — tailored to capture data relevant to the monitoring goals.

Data Acquisition & Processing

Autonomous or manual data capture missions gather comprehensive datasets. Advanced software processes imagery and sensor data, enabling real-time anomaly and trend detection.

Insightful Reporting & Alerts

Deliver geo-tagged, time-stamped reports with annotated imagery, 3D models, vegetation maps, or crowd density analytics. Automated alerts enable proactive response and continuous improvement.

Case Studies

Oil Pipeline ROW Environmental Monitoring

Conducted weekly drone surveillance over 70 km of pipeline right-of-way to monitor vegetation encroachment and detect early pipeline corrosion. Enabled optimized maintenance scheduling and regulatory compliance.

  • Findings: Red zones of vegetation encroachment and corrosion hotspots
  • Result: Prevention of potential leaks and environmental damage
  • Duration: Weekly flights with monthly comprehensive reports


Crowd Density Monitoring at Public Event

Implemented drone monitoring during a large outdoor festival to track crowd movement and density to aid in real-time crowd control and enhance public safety measures without ground intervention.

  • Event attendees: 50,000+
  • Technology: High-res video + AI-based person counting
  • Outcome: Improved emergency response times and crowd flow regulations


Wildlife Habitat Monitoring & Anti-Poaching Surveillance

Used drones to monitor wildlife populations and track poaching threats over a protected reserve. Multispectral analysis supported tracking animal health and vegetation conditions.

  • Area coverage: 400 sq.km
  • Frequency: Monthly surveys
  • Result: Enhanced conservation measures and reduced poaching incidents

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Which industries benefit most from drone-based monitoring?
    Our services support oil & gas, petrochemical, utilities, ports, stadiums, telecom, wildlife conservation, crowd management, and more.
  • Can drones perform monitoring without disrupting active operations?
    Yes — drones operate remotely and safely, capturing data without requiring costly shutdowns or physical site access.
  • What types of sensors are used during monitoring missions?
    Depending on your goals, we deploy multispectral cameras for vegetation and crop health, thermal sensors for heat and leak detection, LiDAR for precise topography, and high-res visual cameras for detailed imagery.
  • How frequently can monitoring flights be scheduled?
    Flights can be scheduled based on your operational and maintenance needs—ranging from daily, weekly, to seasonal monitoring.
  • Are generated reports compatible with GIS, asset management, and compliance systems?
    Absolutely. We deliver geo-tagged, timestamped data and reports in formats compatible with common GIS platforms, CMMS, BIM, and other enterprise tools.
  • How soon can we receive actionable insights and reports?
    Standard delivery times are 24–72 hours post-flight, with options for expedited preliminary reports on urgent inspections.
