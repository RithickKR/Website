Our advanced Ultrasonic Thickness (UT) and Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) services offer precise evaluation of material integrity across various industrial assets. Utilizing cutting-edge drone technology such as the Voliro UAV, we perform safe, efficient, and highly accurate inspections in hazardous and hard-to-reach environments.

Inspection Services Include:

  • Ultrasonic Thickness Measurement for pipelines, storage tanks, and structural components.
  • Corrosion and erosion detection in confined and inaccessible areas.
  • Weld inspection including crack and porosity detection.
  • Detection and localization of leaks in pressure vessels and piping systems.
  • Real-time data acquisition coupled with geo-tagged defect mapping.
  • Comprehensive reporting compliant with industrial and safety standards.

  • How accurate are drone-based UT measurements?

    Our drone-mounted ultrasonic thickness gauges provide measurement accuracy of ±0.01mm, meeting ASTM E797 standards. The system is calibrated before each inspection using certified reference blocks to ensure reliable readings comparable to manual UT inspections.

  • What types of defects can be detected?

    Our NDT system can detect wall thinning, corrosion, cracks, weld defects, coating failures, water ingress in insulation, and thermal anomalies. The combination of visual, ultrasonic, and thermal techniques provides comprehensive defect detection capabilities for most industrial assets.

  • Are your inspection reports accepted by regulatory bodies?

    Yes, our inspection methodology and reporting comply with industry standards including API 510, API 570, and ASME BPVC. Our technicians are certified to ASNT Level II and reports are reviewed by certified inspection engineers to ensure regulatory compliance for insurance and certification requirements.
