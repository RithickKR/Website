Produced a video for a Fortune 500's headquarters including sweeping aerial shots, seasonal documentation, and coordinated aerial/ground testimonials that enhanced the company's online brand presence and recruitment.

  • Content: Employee video interviews, architectural flybys

  • Which locations or settings work best for aerial shoots?

    Aerial shoots shine in open venues, iconic properties, scenic landscapes, and large-scale facilities. We conduct a pre-site assessment to ensure optimal safety and creative results—even for indoor or urban projects.

  • What’s the editing process and delivery time?

    Photography is typically delivered within 48–72 hours, with videos ready in 3–5 business days. We offer fast-track delivery as needed. Edits include color correction, stabilization, and professional finishing with your branding.

  • Can I get both raw and edited content?

    Yes! We provide both fully edited, ready-to-publish images/videos and original raws upon request as part of our professional service packages.

  • Do you handle permits and compliance?

    Our team is fully DGCA (regulatory) compliant and will secure necessary permissions for commercial drone operations at your chosen location.
