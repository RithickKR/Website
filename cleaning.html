  • How do drones compare to traditional cleaning methods?

    Drone-based cleaning typically reduces cleaning time by 70-85% compared to manual methods. They eliminate the need for scaffolding, rope access, or aerial work platforms, significantly reducing setup time and safety risks.

  • What safety measures are implemented during operations?

    All operations follow strict DGCA drone regulations and site-specific risk assessments. Our drones feature collision avoidance systems, redundant flight controllers, and fail-safe protocols.

  • Can you clean while our facility remains operational?

    Yes, in most cases our drone cleaning operations can be conducted without disrupting normal operations. We coordinate with facility management to establish suitable work windows and safety protocols.
