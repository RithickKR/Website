Safeguard Your Power Network with Advanced Drone-Based Powerline Inspections

Our state-of-the-art drone-powered powerline inspection service combines high-resolution imagery and sophisticated thermal scanning to detect faults early and prevent costly outages. Using specialized sensors, AI analytics, and detailed reporting, we provide actionable insights to utility companies and infrastructure managers. Our inspections cover a broad range of powerline assets, including transmission towers, insulators, conductors, and vegetation surroundings, enhancing reliability and safety.

    What we offer:

  • Visual inspection of towers, cross-arms, insulators, and conductors for corrosion, damage, or wear
  • Thermal imaging to locate electrical hotspots, loose connections, and overloaded components
  • Vegetation encroachment analysis identifying potential contact risks and clearance violations
  • Rapid inspection coverage of up to 15 kilometers of powerlines per day, adaptable to various terrains
  • Safe operations reducing worker risk and eliminating the need for dangerous manual climbs and patrols with Flexible scheduling for routine inspections, emergency response, and regulatory compliance checks
  • AI-driven detection and automatic classification of structural and electrical defects and Detailed reporting with geo-tagged photos and thermal maps highlighting damaged and high-risk areas

Key Features

  • High-resolution visual inspection with 42MP RGB imaging
  • Advanced FLIR radiometric thermal sensor for precise hotspot detection
  • Up to 90% faster and safer than manual inspection methods
  • AI-powered real-time defect recognition and automatic classification of faults
  • Comprehensive vegetation encroachment monitoring to prevent outages
  • Integration with asset management systems for streamlined maintenance workflows

Our Powerline Inspection Process

Site Assessment & Safety Planning

Our certified drone pilots conduct a detailed site walkthrough, assess risks including electromagnetic interference, and coordinate with stakeholders to ensure safe and compliant operations.

Aerial Data Capture

Using RTK-enabled drones equipped with high-resolution RGB and thermal cameras, we capture comprehensive imagery of towers, insulators, conductors, and surrounding vegetation from multiple angles.

Data Processing & Analytics

Advanced AI algorithms analyze images and thermal data to detect cracks, corrosion, hot spots, and vegetation encroachment. Our software geotags and classifies defects for precise localization and severity assessment.

Reporting & Recommendations

Detailed inspection reports include annotated imagery, thermal overlays, defect maps, and prioritized maintenance actions to help clients optimize asset health and prevent failures.

Regional Transmission Line Inspection

A 120 km powerline corridor surveyed, identifying 37 critical issues including insulator damage and conductor corrosion. The project saved over 80% of the time compared to traditional manual inspections.

  • Length inspected: 120 km
  • Defects found: 37 in total
  • Time savings: 80% over manual methods
  • Impact: Prevented multiple unplanned outages

Substation Thermal Inspection

Thermal imaging detected 5 faulty connectors and 3 overheated transformers in a high-voltage substation, allowing targeted repairs before failures occurred.

  • Faults detected: Connectors and transformers
  • Report turnaround: 48 hours
  • Benefit: Reduced downtime and maintenance costs

Vegetation Management Survey

Automated drone surveys mapped vegetation encroachment on transmission corridors, aiding in efficient clearance planning and wildfire risk reduction.

  • Area covered: 50 km of ROW
  • Technology: Multispectral imaging and AI analysis
  • Outcome: Enhanced safety and regulatory compliance
  • What level of detail can drones achieve in powerline inspection?

    Our drones capture high-resolution images capable of revealing cracks as small as 2 mm and thermal anomalies with 0.05°C sensitivity.

  • Can inspections be performed on energized lines?

    Yes, our operations maintain safe distances and operate under strict protocols, enabling live inspections without disrupting service.

  • What types of reports do you provide?

    We deliver comprehensive reports with GPS-tagged defects, annotated thermal imaging, and prioritized maintenance recommendations.

  • How fast do you deliver inspection reports?

    Standard reports are delivered within 48-72 hours after inspection, with expedited options available for urgent cases.

  • Are your inspections compliant with industry standards?

    Yes, all inspections comply with DGCA and industry-specific regulations, suitable for audit and insurance purposes.
