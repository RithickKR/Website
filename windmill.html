Our Inspection Process

Pre-inspection Planning

Our team analyzes wind conditions, weather forecasts, and site layout to ensure safety and optimal drone performance. Every inspection begins with detailed planning—mapping out the flight paths to maximize data coverage while minimizing the risk posed by gusts, turbulence, and structural obstacles. Regulatory compliance and safety protocols are reviewed before every mission.

Multi-sensor Capture

Using drones equipped with ultra-high resolution cameras and sensitive thermal sensors, we capture comprehensive imagery of every blade, hub, and tower. Visual imaging reveals cracks, erosion, lightning strikes, or surface contamination, while thermal scans expose hidden hot spots or internal defects not visible to the eye. All turbine components are recorded from multiple precise angles.

AI-powered Analysis

Powered by machine learning, our software rapidly reviews all data to automatically detect and classify any anomalies or damage. The AI flags defects—such as cracks, delamination, or lightning impacts—and rates them by severity, minimizing human error and allowing large wind farms to be processed efficiently. This ensures no defect is missed and urgent issues are highlighted for action.

Reporting

You receive a comprehensive, industry-compliant report featuring annotated images, thermal overlays, and a prioritized repair list. Each finding includes detailed location, type, and severity, plus actionable recommendations for targeted maintenance planning. Reports align with IEC 61400 standards and can include interactive 3D models for your asset management systems.

  • How often should wind turbines be inspected?

    Industry standards recommend bi-annual inspections, with additional checks after severe weather events.

  • What types of damage can you detect?

    Our system detects leading edge erosion, lightning strikes, delamination, cracks, surface contamination, and internal structural issues via thermal imaging.

  • How long does an inspection take?

    A standard three-blade turbine inspection takes approximately 45-60 minutes under optimal weather conditions.
