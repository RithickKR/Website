Inspection and Monitoring Services

Our professional inspection and monitoring solutions help you maintain safety standards, ensure compliance, and prevent costly downtime through advanced technology.

Chimney Inspection

Comprehensive industrial stack assessments without risky climbs. Detailed inspections identify cracks, liner damage, and structural integrity issues at height.

Pipeline Leak Detection

Advanced thermal imaging identifies leaks in oil and gas pipelines before they become environmental hazards. Our technology detects even minute temperature variations.

Mining Site Inspection

Comprehensive aerial surveillance of mining operations. Monitor stockpile volumes, equipment placement, and compliance with safety regulations from above.

Solar Panel Inspection

Thermal imaging identifies faulty panels and efficiency issues. Our aerial inspections cover large solar farms quickly, pinpointing hotspots and defects.

Project Monitoring & Inspection

Regular aerial documentation of construction progress with detailed analytics. Track milestones, verify specifications, and ensure project timelines are met with precision.

Highway/Traffic Monitoring

Aerial surveillance for traffic management and infrastructure planning. Monitor congestion, incident response, and road conditions without disrupting flow.

Structural Damage Assessment

High-resolution imaging for bridges, buildings, and infrastructure. Identify cracks, corrosion, and structural weaknesses without costly scaffolding or disruption.

Internal Boiler Inspection

Compact drone solutions for confined space entry. Visual documentation of internal surfaces identifies corrosion and material degradation without human entry risks.

Wind Turbine Inspection

Detailed blade and nacelle inspections without production stoppage. High-resolution imaging detects surface damage, erosion, and structural issues at height.

Powerline Inspection

Detailed assessment of transmission infrastructure. Identify damaged insulators, vegetation encroachment, and structural issues without power disruption.

Surveying and Mapping Services

Precision mapping and surveying solutions for accurate spatial data collection and analysis.

Advanced Photogrammetry

High-resolution drone photography for creating detailed 2D and 3D maps with centimeter-level accuracy for various applications.

2D & 3D Design Modelling

Digital representations for planning and visualization that transform raw data into actionable insights for architects and engineers.

LiDAR Survey & Analysis

High-resolution terrain modeling through vegetation and challenging environments using advanced laser scanning technology.

Orthomosaic & Contour Lines

Geometrically corrected aerial images that create seamless maps with accurate elevation information for engineering projects.

Volumetric & Stockpile Monitoring

Accurate material volume measurement for mining, construction, and agriculture with regular change detection reporting.

DSM/DTM/DEM Generation

Creation of detailed digital surface, terrain, and elevation models for hydrological analysis and infrastructure planning.

Large Scale Mapping

Comprehensive area mapping for regional planning, agriculture management, and environmental conservation projects.

Bathymetric Survey

Underwater terrain mapping for coastal management, dredging operations, and underwater infrastructure planning.
