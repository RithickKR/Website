Survey Methodology

Project Scoping & Planning

We start by consulting with your team to define the mapping goals and select the right deliverables. Critical ground control points are identified and regulatory protocols (permits/airspace) are managed seamlessly.

Field Data Collection

Our drone and GNSS teams deploy to capture high-resolution aerial imagery and georeferenced data, even in challenging or remote environments.

Geo-processing & Quality Checks

Collected data is rigorously processed into dense point clouds, orthomosaics, and precise terrain models, all reviewed for accuracy and completeness.

Deliverables & Support

We provide outputs tailored to your needs—orthomaps, CAD files, GIS layers, cross-sections, and more—along with expert support for integration into your workflows.

Utility Corridor Mapping

Mapped a 40km route for new underground utilities, providing detailed topographic data and section profiles that empowered rapid design approvals and reduced field time by 70%.

  • Length: 40km
  • Deliverables: DXF (CAD), SHP (GIS), profile reports
  • Delivery: 3 business days


Large-Scale Land Development

Boundary, DTM, and contour survey over 120 hectares for a new residential township, with ±3cm accuracy and seamless handover into the client’s BIM pipeline.

  • Area: 120 hectares
  • Accuracy: ±3cm
  • Output: CAD, GIS, Orthomosaics, Volume Reports
  • What kinds of surveys do you provide?

    We offer topographic, boundary, site layout, volume, corridor/route, and utility network surveys for infrastructure, engineering, utilities, and environmental clients.

  • How fast are your deliverables?

    Most projects are completed and delivered within 2–4 business days, depending on area size and complexity. Large or technically challenging surveys may require additional time.

  • Is your survey data legally valid?

    Yes, all output meets national/regional survey standards, and we provide signed documentation and reference sheets required for compliance or permits.

  • Can you survey forested or water-rich areas?

    Our drones and GNSS workflows can effectively map terrain under moderate vegetation and near water bodies, using adaptive sensors and optimized flight planning.
