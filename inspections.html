Transform Industrial Asset Management with Drone Inspections

Leverage advanced drone and sensor technology for thorough, safe, and cost-effective inspections of critical external infrastructure: oil and gas fields, pipelines, port equipment, storage tanks, stadium lighting towers, cell towers, construction sites, and more. Our DGCA-certified operators eliminate manual risk and deliver unparalleled asset insight.

What We Monitor

  • Pipelines & Oil/Gas Facilities: Vegetation encroachment, corrosion, thermal anomaly detection, and Right-of-Way (ROW) compliance.
  • Industrial & Environmental Assets: Storage tanks, ports, stadium lights, telecom towers, and construction sites monitoring with high-resolution imaging.
  • Crowd & Public Safety Monitoring: Live event density tracking and crowd flow management with AI-powered video analytics.
  • Wildlife & Habitat Surveillance: Population tracking, anti-poaching monitoring, and vegetation health using multispectral drones.
  • Environmental Compliance: Spill detection, chemical leak identification, and soil contamination mapping.

Key Features

  • Multi-sensor drones including thermal, multispectral, LiDAR, and HD video
  • Automated anomaly and change detection using AI analytics
  • Geo-tagged, timestamped high-resolution reports with annotated images and 3D models
  • Flexible scheduling for continuous, seasonal, or event-based monitoring
  • Compliant with all relevant DGCA and industry regulations for safe operations

Applications

  • Oil & Gas: Pipeline corrosion and leak monitoring, flare stack thermal surveillance
  • Ports: Equipment integrity, cargo yard condition, and marine asset health
  • Stadiums: Structural health monitoring of lighting and cables, crowd safety assessments
  • Telecommunications: Post-storm tower integrity and antenna inspections
  • Construction: Progress tracking, safety compliance, and site monitoring
  • Environmental: Wildlife tracking, habitat health, and spill detection
  • Crowd: Festivals, concerts, and large gatherings crowd density mapping and management

Monitoring Workflow

1

Planning & Risk Assessment

Define specific monitoring objectives for industrial sites, wildlife habitats, or crowd events. Assess environmental constraints, airspace regulations, and safety protocols.

2

Customized Sensor Deployment

Deploy drones equipped with the right sensors—thermal, multispectral, LiDAR, or HD visual cameras—to effectively capture relevant data for your monitoring goals.

3

Data Acquisition & Processing

Conduct autonomous or manual drone missions to collect comprehensive datasets. Process imagery and sensor data with advanced AI and analytics to detect real-time anomalies and trends.

4

Insightful Reporting & Alerts

Deliver geo-tagged, timestamped reports featuring annotated images, 3D models, vegetation health maps, or crowd density analytics. Automated alerts support proactive responses and continuous improvement.

Case Studies

Oil Pipeline ROW Environmental Monitoring

Weekly drone flights over 70 km of pipeline right-of-way tracked vegetation encroachment and early corrosion, enabling optimized maintenance and regulatory compliance.

  • Findings: Vegetation overgrowth and corrosion hotspots
  • Outcome: Prevented leaks and environmental impact
  • Flight Frequency: Weekly, with monthly comprehensive reports

Crowd Density Monitoring at Festival

Deployed drones to monitor a 50,000+ person outdoor festival, estimating crowd density and movement patterns in real-time for enhanced public safety management.

  • Technology: High-res video coupled with AI-based person counting
  • Impact: Improved emergency response and crowd flow control

Wildlife & Anti-Poaching Surveillance

Monthly drone surveys over a protected reserve monitored animal populations and detected poaching threats, supported by multispectral vegetation health mapping.

  • Area: 400 sq.km reserve
  • Result: Reduced poaching and improved conservation efforts

Industrial Spill & Emission Monitoring

Thermal and visual drone assessments at chemical storage sites detected minor emission leaks and spills, enabling rapid remediation and regulatory compliance.

  • Scope: 10 hazardous material tanks inspected
  • Benefit: Early leak detection and environmental protection

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Which industries benefit most from drone-based monitoring?
    Our services support oil & gas, petrochemical, utilities, ports, stadiums, telecom, wildlife conservation, crowd management, and more.
  • Can drones perform monitoring without disrupting active operations?
    Yes, drones operate remotely and safely, avoiding costly shutdowns or on-site hazards.
  • What types of sensors are used during monitoring missions?
    We use multispectral, thermal, LiDAR, and high-res visual cameras, tailored to your monitoring needs.
  • How often can monitoring flights be scheduled?
    Scheduling is flexible—daily, weekly, monthly, or seasonal—according to operational requirements.
  • Are generated reports compatible with GIS and asset management systems?
    Absolutely. Reports and data integrate with GIS, CMMS, BIM, and other enterprise tools.
  • How soon are reports delivered?
    Standard delivery is 24–72 hours post-flight, with expedited reports available for urgent needs.
