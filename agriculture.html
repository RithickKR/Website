Revolutionize Agriculture with Precision Drone Spraying & Crop Analytics

Harness the power of advanced agricultural drones for efficient crop spraying, health assessment, and data-driven farm management. Our DGCA-certified UAV operations deliver precise spraying, optimize input costs, and help boost yields—safely, sustainably, and quickly across large acreages.

What We Offer

  • Precision Crop Spraying: Targeted, even application of pesticides, fungicides, and fertilizers with minimal wastage and up to 90% water savings compared to traditional methods.
  • Variable Rate Application: Intelligent zone-by-zone dosing to place exactly the right input where it’s needed—reduce chemical use, improve plant health, and mitigate resistance.
  • Multi-Spectral Crop Analysis: NDVI & multispectral mapping detects plant health issues early—identify stress, nutrient deficiencies, pest outbreaks, and irrigation problems before they impact yields.
  • Crop Stand Count & Mapping: Rapid, high-resolution surveys to count plant population, assess emergence, and generate actionable acreage and gap maps.
  • Soil & Moisture Mapping: Analyze moisture variation and soil texture for data-driven irrigation planning.
  • Pasture & Plantation Management: Get canopy height models, weed maps, and monitor perennial crops, orchards, and forestry plots.

Key Features

  • 20L–40L Payload capacity for efficient large-field spraying
  • RTK-enabled flight for pinpoint accuracy (±10 cm)
  • Automated flight plans and adaptive spraying algorithms
  • Data outputs for farm management platforms, GIS, and compliance
  • Reduce labor costs and operator exposure to chemicals

Typical Applications

  • Paddy, wheat, and row crops
  • Horticulture: vineyards, orchards, sugarcane, banana
  • Plantation forests, cash crops, and seed production
  • Tea, coffee, cotton, and vegetable farms
  • Pest/disease scouting and rapid response spraying

  • What crops can benefit from drone spraying?

    Our services are optimized for row crops (rice, wheat, corn), orchards, vineyards, and specialty high-value crops. The technology is particularly effective for hard-to-access areas and tall crops.

  • How does the analysis help improve yields?

    Our multispectral analysis identifies problem areas 2-3 weeks before visible to the human eye, allowing for early intervention. Clients typically see 15-25% yield improvements through timely pest/disease management and nutrient optimization.

  • Are your spraying operations compliant with regulations?

    Yes, we maintain all necessary certifications for agricultural drone operations, including DGCA approval, pesticide applicator licenses, and compliance with local agricultural departments.
