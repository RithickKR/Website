SURVEY DRONE

DOPO UAV is a DGCA type certified drone, engineered to deliver high-quality aerial imagery, precise spatial data, and reliable performance for professional applications in land surveying, mapping, construction, infrastructure monitoring, environmental analysis, precision agriculture, and more.

With exceptional endurance and advanced payload options, the DOPO UAV empowers businesses to complete large surveying projects in a single flight while meeting regulatory compliance and highest accuracy standards.

  • Up to 49 minutes flight time (AUW 4.7 kg)

  • 24MP CMOS sensor, high-accuracy aerial imaging (up to 2.5cm/pixel GSD)

  • Communication range up to 5 km

  • PPK support for up to 5cm XYZ accuracy

  • BVLOS ready, 120m AGL, up to 3000m MSL operation

  • Custom Li-Po battery for maximum efficiency

DOPO UAV is a rugged, industry-ready drone platform certified by DGCA (India), and designed for high-precision data acquisition in challenging work environments. Its superior imaging and GNSS accuracy, user-friendly Android TAS GCS, and BVLOS capability make it an ideal choice for professional surveyors, engineers, and industrial project managers.

Whether you need precise mapping, construction monitoring, industrial inspections, or agricultural analytics, DOPO UAV delivers reliable and repeatable results with minimal downtime and robust wind tolerance (up to 12m/s).

  • Surveying & Mapping: Rapid, high-resolution land surveys, topographic maps, and urban planning datasets in a single flight (up to 1.2 sq. km coverage).
  • Mining & Aggregates: Stockpile volume measurement, site audits, and safe, remote exploration of mining areas.
  • Construction: Construction progress monitoring, site inspection, documentation, and stakeholder reporting.
  • Industrial Inspection: Infrastructure monitoring and defect detection in utilities, energy, and large industrial assets.
  • Precision Agriculture: Crop health monitoring, farm mapping, and data-driven agri-decision support.
  • Environmental Assessment: Land use mapping, environmental impact studies, and disaster documentation.
  • Certification: DGCA Type Certified, WPC Approved
  • Maximum Endurance: Up to 49 minutes
  • Maximum Coverage: Up to 1.2 sq. km per flight
  • Maximum Altitude: 120m AGL (compliant), operation up to 3000m MSL
  • Wind Tolerance: 12 m/s
  • Weight (AUW): 4.7 kg
  • Energy: Customized Li-Po Battery
  • Communication Range: Up to 5 km (FHSS 2.4 – 2.482 GHz)
  • Cruise Speed / Max Speed: 9–10 m/s (cruise), 24 m/s (max)
  • Image Sensor: 24.3 MP CMOS sensor, high accuracy & clarity
  • Accuracy: Up to 2.5cm/pixel GSD, PPK achieves ≤5cm X,Y,Z accuracy
  • Ground Control: Android TAS GCS Platform
  • BVLOS: Ready out-of-the-box
