LOGISTICS & DELIVERY DRONES

TAS L-series UAV is an advanced logistics drone designed to efficiently deliver a wide variety of cargo to diverse and challenging destinations. Whether the mission is humanitarian, commercial, or military, the L-series is engineered to operate with reliability and precision.

With both low- and high-altitude variants, the L-series adapts to different terrains and delivery scenarios—from urban last-mile ecommerce to life-saving healthcare supply drops and critical military resupply in the field. Its smart design enables fast deployment, secure payload handling, and ease of operation with Android TAS GCS.

  • Payload capacity: 5 - 40 kg

  • Adaptable for commercial, humanitarian, or military deliveries

  • Up to 30 minutes flight time

  • Remote ID: ASTM Standard via cellular; Primary C2: 2.4 GHz, Secondary: 4G telematics

  • Operation at 120m AGL; cruise: 8 m/s, max: 12 m/s

  • IP45 ingress protection, wind tolerance up to 14 m/s

TAS L-series UAV is built for demanding delivery operations, excelling in long-range, time-sensitive, or high-volume missions. Its robust payload handling and advanced telemetry enable secure transport of parcels, food, medicine, equipment, or humanitarian aid to urban, remote, or hard-to-reach locations, even in adverse weather.

Its modular design allows for easy reconfiguration between low- and high-altitude variants, making it suitable for civilian, logistics, healthcare, and military use cases. Operators benefit from intuitive controls through the Android TAS GCS, ensuring safe and efficient missions every time.

  • Logistics & Last Mile: Direct parcel delivery from distribution hubs to customer doorsteps or remote settlements, overcoming traffic and terrain barriers.
  • E-Commerce: Rapid, contactless delivery of consumer goods for online retailers, optimizing last-mile efficiency and reducing turnaround time.
  • Healthcare Delivery: Transportation of medical supplies, blood, vaccines, and emergency medical equipment to clinics, hospitals, or disaster-hit areas.
  • Humanitarian Aid: Supply drops to isolated communities or emergency zones with food, water, medicine, or communications gear.
  • Defense Supply: On-demand delivery of rations, ammunition, and mission-critical hardware to troops in the field.
  • Altitude: 120m AGL
  • Payload: 5 – 40 kg delivery
  • Flight Time: Up to 30 minutes
  • Wind Tolerance: 14 m/s
  • Ingress Protection: IP45
  • Support Platform: Android TAS GCS
  • C2 Link: Primary: 2.4 GHz / Secondary: 4G telematics
  • Remote ID: ASTM Standard via cellular communication
  • Flying Speed: Cruise: 8 m/s / Max: 12 m/s
  • Variants: Low- and high-altitude models for operational flexibility
  • Use Case Examples: Last-mile logistics, e-commerce, medical & humanitarian supply, military resupply
