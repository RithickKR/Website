nimble-i will shock you with its performance and price tag. Boasting stunning looks, rugged build, and segment-first advanced features—including 10x zoom camera in the micro-UAV category, forward-facing lidar, and long-range digital transmission—nimble-i is set to carry on TAS’s legacy for innovation.

The platform offers hot-swappable payloads & batteries, high precision GPS, and on-board AI features, making it a versatile and reliable choice for multi-mission deployments in the toughest conditions. Palm-launch capable, nimble-i is truly rapid and single-operator ready.

nimble-i redefines expectations in the micro-UAV category, with performance and features once reserved for larger, costlier drones. The default payload includes a 4K RGB camera with 10x optical zoom (and optional 720P/640x512 thermal), supported by high precision navigation and on-board artificial intelligence for smart detection tasks.*

Its rugged construction, long endurance, and hot-swappable payloads and batteries maximize operational uptime. Designed for fast-paced missions, nimble-i can be deployed in seconds and recovered by palm, making it ideal for rapid-response, single-operator tasks in the field.

  • Disaster Management: Real-time situational awareness, search & rescue, post-incident assessment.
  • Security & Surveillance: Perimeter monitoring, patrol, and threat detection in sensitive sites.
  • Crowd Monitoring: Crowd density analysis, event security, and public order support.
  • Forest & Wildfire: Early fire detection, rapid area mapping, and incident documentation.
  • General Tactical Use: Any mission requiring a rugged UAV with powerful optics, AI, and rapid, hand-held operation.
  • Endurance: Up to 35 minutes
  • Payload: 2 hot-swappable payload slots
  • Default Camera: 4K RGB (1080P HD 10x Zoom), optional 720P 640×512 IR*
  • Altitude: 2000 MSL / 120m AGL
  • Communication: Digital link, up to 3 km
  • On-Board AI: Embedded AI for smart detection*
  • GPS: High precision positioning
  • Deployment: Single-person rapid palm launch/recovery
  • Wind Tolerance: Up to 10 m/s
  • Build: Tough, rugged construction

*Features/payloads may vary by configuration—ask for details.
