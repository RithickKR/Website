SURVEILLANCE DRONE

SWAT is a state-of-the-art small unmanned aerial system (sUAS), purpose built to deliver exceptional endurance, rapid deployment, and advanced surveillance for critical missions. With best-in-class flight time and payload flexibility, SWAT is ready for single-operator, field operations—anytime, anywhere.

SWAT features field-swappable, gyro-stabilized EO/IR payloads (4K HDR, 10x optical zoom, thermal imaging) to meet any real-time observation requirement. Secure all-digital transmission with AES-256 encryption and up to 10 km range provides unmatched operational reach for defense, disaster, and security missions.

  • Up to 55 minutes endurance (AUW 4.2 kg)

  • Field-swappable, 3-axis gyro stabilized EO/IR, 4K HDR, 10x Zoom, and Thermal payloads

  • Up to 10 km digital range with AES-256 encryption

  • Portable tablet-based handheld controller (Android TAS GCS)

  • Rapid, single-person deployment and operation

  • Wind tolerance up to 14 m/s, Li-ion Battery

SWAT is engineered for robust, multi-domain operations—disaster response, law enforcement, security surveillance, and crowd management—where mission success depends on speed, coverage, and data security.

The system supports plug-and-play EO/IR and thermal payloads, all controlled from an intuitive handheld Android TAS Ground Control Station (GCS). AES-256 encrypted digital link keeps your video and command links fully secure even in high-interference environments. The result is a mission-ready UAS—easy to transport, quick to deploy, and suitable for all-weather use.

  • Disaster Management: Real-time situational awareness, site assessment, search and rescue, and aerial mapping post-natural disasters.
  • Security & Surveillance: Perimeter patrol, event security, and high-value asset monitoring for defense, police, and private agencies.
  • Crowd Monitoring: Crowd density and movement analysis, riot control oversight, and public event management.
  • Forest & Wildfire Operations: Early fire detection, hotspot mapping, and patrols in remote or difficult terrains.
  • General Aerial Observation: Infrastructure inspection, border surveillance, and law enforcement.
  • Altitude: 120m AGL, BVLOS Ready
  • Weight: AUW 4.2 kg
  • Flight Time: Up to 55 minutes
  • Wind Tolerance: 14 m/s
  • Energy: Li-ion Battery
  • Support Platform: Android TAS GCS (Tablet Handheld Controller)
  • Certifications: NPNT, WPC Approved
  • C2 Link: FHSS 2.4 – 2.482 GHz, up to 10 km range, AES-256 encrypted
  • Flying Speed: Cruise: 9–10 m/s, Max: 24 m/s
  • Image Sensors: EO (10x optical zoom, 4K HDR), IR (thermal)
  • Peso: Field-swappable, 3-axis gyro stabilization
  • Deployment: Rapid, single person setup and launch
