TAVAS UAS is engineered for versatile, high-risk surveillance and inspection missions—combining robust aerial performance, smart payloads, and advanced AI analytics. Its elegant aerodynamics and robust build offer IP54 all-weather protection, making it highly durable for continuous urban and field use.

Real-time 10x zoom, full day/night video, and suite of AI-enabled recognition features make TAVAS the ideal choice for agencies and enterprises aiming for maximum situational awareness and proactive response.

  • Public Safety & Crowd Monitoring: Real-time crowd detection and movement analysis for events, urban gatherings, and emergency evacuations.
  • Critical Infrastructure Surveillance: Continuous monitoring of strategic sites and utilities with automatic threat/object identification.
  • Traffic & Vehicle Analytics: Day/night vehicle tracking, number plate reading, and incident documentation for law enforcement or city management.
  • Foreign Object & Asset Security: Comprehensive surveillance of restricted areas, airfields, or industrial sites with AI-driven alerts.
  • Disaster Response: Fast mapping and ongoing assessment with night-capable, long-range visuals and analytics.
  • General Aerial Observation: Any advanced surveillance or inspection requiring extended flight, precision imaging, and safety-first automation.
  • Altitude: 120m AGL operational ceiling
  • Flying Time: Up to 45 minutes
  • Energy: Custom Li-PO Battery
  • Speed: Cruise 10m/s, Max 26m/s
  • Ingress Protection: IP54
  • Range: Maximum 3km (FHSS 2.4–2.482 GHz C2 link)
  • Core Payload: 10x optical zoom camera with AI analytics, 6-side lidar obstacle avoidance
  • Support Platform: Compatible with ground-based long-range detection and analytics systems
  • Day/Night Capabilities: Full-spectrum surveillance with advanced sensors
  • Smart Features: Crowd detection, vehicle recognition, foreign object identification, number plate reading (AI-driven)
