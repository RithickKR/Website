Our Inspection Process

Site Assessment & Safety Planning

Our certified drone pilots conduct a thorough site walkthrough and risk analysis to identify potential hazards, environmental factors, and compliance requirements. We customize flight plans that prioritize safety and maximize data collection accuracy.

Aerial Inspection with High-Precision Drones

Using RTK-enabled drones fitted with high-resolution optical zoom cameras and radiometric thermal sensors, we capture comprehensive 360° visual and thermal data. This allows us to detect structural anomalies, heat leakages, or other defects with micron-level detail.

Expert Data Processing & Analysis

Our team leverages advanced software to process imagery and thermal data. We analyze visual patterns, temperature differentials, and geometrical deformations to identify cracks, corrosion, insulation failures, and hotspots.

Detailed Reporting & Recommendations

Clients receive a comprehensive report encompassing high-definition images, annotated thermal maps, and actionable insights. Our recommendations are tailored to extend the chimney's operational life, improve safety, and optimize maintenance schedules.

Power Plant Chimney Inspection — Lightning Damage Assessment

In an emergency scenario, our drone inspection was deployed within 24 hours after a lightning strike on a 250-meter chimney. Using thermal and visual imaging, we quickly identified critical structural damage including fractures and heat anomaly spots suspicious for insulation failure.

  • Chimney Height: 250 meters
  • Inspection Time: 24 hours turnaround
  • Outcome: Delivered detailed damage assessment enabling rapid repairs, preventing extended downtime


Cement Factory Annual Environmental Compliance Survey

Conducted a regulatory inspection of three industrial chimneys to identify emission leaks and ensure compliance. Our drone-based thermal surveys detected unreported hotspots indicating potential emission breaches, leading to corrective action before regulatory penalties.

  • Structures Inspected: 3 chimneys/stacks
  • Findings: Emission leak detected through thermal anomalies
  • Deliverables: Preventive maintenance plan with thermal imagery and targeted repair recommendations
  • Is drone chimney inspection safe?

    Absolutely. Our drone inspections remove the need for hazardous scaffolding and manual climbing, significantly reducing human risk during chimney assessments. Prior to flight, we conduct extensive safety planning to adhere to all regulations.

  • What equipment do you use?

    We deploy advanced RTK-enabled drones equipped with high-resolution optical zoom cameras for visual clarity and radiometric thermal cameras to capture temperature variations. This combination ensures precise identification of structural defects and thermal inefficiencies.

  • Can you inspect active chimneys?

    Yes, depending on the operational temperature and safety thresholds, we can perform inspections on active chimneys without disrupting operations. Our drones withstand typical operational environments and gather data safely from elevated heat zones.

  • How fast can inspection reports be delivered?

    Standard turnaround for detailed reports ranges from 24 to 48 hours post-inspection, depending on the complexity and scale of the project. We prioritize rapid delivery for urgent inspections such as emergency damage assessments.

  • Do you provide maintenance recommendations?

    Yes, our reports include comprehensive recommendations for maintenance, repairs, or upgrades based on detected defects and potential risk areas. This assists your engineering team in planning cost-effective, preventive maintenance.
