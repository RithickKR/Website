Official Reseller & Distributor of TAS — India's Leading DGCA-Certified Drone Manufacturer in Partnership with NeoSky

TAS is proud to introduce India's first DGCA-certified drone manufacturer, duly licensed to produce drones for the Ministry of Defense, with DGCA Type Certification, and recognized under the Government of India's Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. We are the pioneers in India to successfully complete BVLOS trials and contribute to DGCA’s regulatory framework.

Our extensive product suite spans land mapping, surveillance, cargo transport, inspection, agriculture, disaster management, and beyond — featuring industry-leading models such as DOPO, TACT, Nimble, L-Series, Defender, and more.

In partnership with NeoSky, the parent company and an authorized DGCA-approved RPTO (Remote Pilot Training Organization), we proudly serve as their official reseller and distributor, delivering cutting-edge drone technology and comprehensive services across India.

TAS is committed to innovation and scalability, poised to revolutionize the future of aerial mobility solutions.
