Data We Collect

In the course of providing our services, we may collect:

  • Client contact information (name, email, phone, company details)
  • Flight operation data from drone sensors and cameras
  • Geospatial data collected during surveys and inspections
  • Technical logs and maintenance records for our equipment
  • Website usage data through analytics cookies

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Deliver and improve our DAAS solutions
  • Generate analytical reports for clients
  • Maintain safety and operational standards
  • Communicate service updates and security notices
  • Comply with aviation regulations and industry standards

Data Security

We implement military-grade encryption for all flight data and client information. Our security measures include:

  • 256-bit SSL encryption for data transfers
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication for system access
  • Secure cloud storage with geographic redundancy

Third-Party Data Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Certified aviation authorities as required by regulation
  • Cloud service providers under strict NDAs
  • Technical partners for specialized data processing
  • Legal authorities when required by law

We never sell client data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain operational data for 7 years to comply with aviation regulations. Client reports are maintained for 3 years post-service unless otherwise requested.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of non-essential data
  • Object to specific data processing activities
  • Receive your data in portable format

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential and analytical cookies. Third-party services include:

  • Google Analytics for usage statistics
  • Hotjar for user experience optimization
  • Drone flight planning software cookies
