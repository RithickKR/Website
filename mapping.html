3D Topographic Mapping

  • Home
  • Services
  • 3D Mapping

With our 3D topographic mapping, you get actionable terrain insight—delivered fast. Our fleet of advanced drones captures both LiDAR and photogrammetry data, enabling highly detailed 3D surface models for all landscape types. Perfect for construction, mining, flood risk, and urban planning.

What We Offer:

  • LiDAR, RTK/PPK, and oblique photogrammetry options
  • 5cm vertical/3cm horizontal precision achievable
  • Seamless GIS, CAD, and BIM integration
  • Expert QA and project consulting included
  • True-to-scale 3D surface and terrain models (DSM/DTM)
  • Colorized and classified point clouds for engineering
  • Accurate volumetric analysis (stockpiles, cut & fill)
  • Contour mapping and cross-section extraction
  • High-resolution orthomosaic visualization

Ideal For:

  • Earthworks & pre-construction planning
  • Open-pit mining & aggregate management
  • Urban land development & flood risk mapping
  • Environmental monitoring & compliance

Our Mapping Process

Precision Planning

Define project goals and set GNSS control for ultra-accurate referencing.

Automated Aerial Capture

Deploy RTK/PPK drones to collect dense, high-overlap imagery for every contour and feature.

3D Data Modelling

Generate colored point clouds and terrain models using photogrammetry and LiDAR, revealing true site shape.

Seamless Handover

Deliver ready-to-use CAD, GIS, and analytics files with a clear, actionable report.

Iron Ore Mine Stockpile Mapping

Rapid, safe, and precise 3D mapping of all mine stockpiles. Delivery of volume and elevation reports supported both inventory reconciliation and operational planning.

  • Site: 250ha, weekly monitoring
  • Accuracy: ±0.3% volume
  • Turnaround: Results in 48 hours


Highway Alignment & Earthwork

Topographic mapping over 15km enables quick cut/fill calculations for engineering teams, streamlining highway buildout.

  • Corridor: 15km long, 1:500 mapping
  • Product: Digital Terrain Models in Civil3D
  • Outcome: Reduced ground survey time by 70%

    We routinely achieve 5cm vertical accuracy thanks to advanced RT
