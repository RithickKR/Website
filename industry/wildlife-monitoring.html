  • How do drones minimize disturbance to wildlife?

    Our drones operate at optimal altitudes (50-100m) where they are virtually silent to most wildlife. We use specialized quiet rotors and maintain safe distances to ensure minimal behavioral impact while collecting high-quality data.

  • What advantages does thermal imaging provide?

    Thermal imaging allows us to detect animals day and night, through dense foliage, and in challenging terrain. It's particularly effective for nocturnal species and in anti-poaching operations where conventional methods are limited.

  • Can you monitor small or elusive species?

    Yes, our high-resolution cameras and AI-powered image recognition can identify and track even small species. We've successfully monitored everything from nesting birds to small mammals and reptiles.
