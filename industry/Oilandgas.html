Comprehensive Oil & Gas Solutions

Pipeline Integrity Management

Our drones conduct comprehensive pipeline inspections that combine visual assessment with advanced leak detection capabilities. Using optical gas imaging (OGI) sensors, we identify methane and other hydrocarbon leaks along pipeline routes with unprecedented efficiency. This technology detects emissions invisible to the naked eye, enabling early intervention before leaks escalate into safety or environmental incidents.

Beyond leak detection, our high-resolution cameras document pipeline conditions, supporting structures, and right-of-way encroachments. We identify corrosion, coating damage, and potential geohazards such as erosion or ground movement. The aerial perspective provides context that ground patrols cannot achieve, revealing patterns and relationships across extensive pipeline networks.

For construction projects, our drones monitor pipeline installation progress and verify burial depth compliance. We document as-built conditions and compare them against design specifications, ensuring quality control throughout the construction lifecycle. This comprehensive documentation supports regulatory compliance and reduces disputes during handover.

Facility Inspection and Maintenance

Refineries and processing plants benefit from our drone-based inspection solutions that access difficult-to-reach structures without scaffolding or production shutdowns. Using high-resolution zoom cameras and thermal imaging, we inspect columns, vessels, tanks, and piping for corrosion, insulation damage, and thermal anomalies. This approach reduces inspection costs by up to 50% while improving data quality.

Flare stack inspections represent a particularly valuable application of drone technology. Our specialized systems capture detailed imagery of flare tips, structural supports, and refractory lining without requiring shutdowns or erecting scaffolding. Thermal imaging identifies hot spots and combustion efficiency issues, while visual inspection documents structural integrity and corrosion.

For storage tank inspections, our drones conduct external roof and shell assessments while minimizing confined space entries. We identify corrosion, coating failures, and structural issues with high-resolution imagery. Internal inspections using explosion-proof drones eliminate the need for human entry, significantly reducing safety risks and downtime.

Offshore and Marine Operations

Offshore platforms utilize our drone services for comprehensive structural inspections that minimize personnel exposure to hazardous environments. We inspect jacket structures, decks, helipads, and living quarters using high-resolution cameras and thermal sensors. The aerial perspective enables rapid assessment of hard-to-access areas without requiring rope access or scaffolding.

Subsea inspection capabilities include above-water assessment of risers, mooring systems, and floating structures. Our drones capture detailed imagery of splash zones and tidal areas that are challenging for divers or ROVs. By conducting inspections without vessel support in many cases, we significantly reduce operational costs and carbon footprint.

Marine terminal operations benefit from our drone-based monitoring of loading arms, jetties, and storage facilities. We detect hydrocarbon sheens on water surfaces, monitor vessel operations, and inspect inaccessible structures. This comprehensive surveillance enhances safety, environmental protection, and operational efficiency in critical marine environments.

Workflow for Oil & Gas Operations

  1. Scope Definition: Identifying inspection objectives and areas
  2. Sensor Selection: Choosing appropriate payloads (visual, thermal, OGI)
  3. Safety Planning: Developing JSA and flight protocols
  4. Field Operations: Conducting drone flights with certified pilots
  5. Data Collection: Capturing high-resolution imagery and sensor data
  6. Data Processing: Analyzing and correlating multiple data streams
  7. Anomaly Detection: Identifying leaks, corrosion, and structural issues
  8. Reporting: Delivering actionable insights and prioritized actions

Business Benefits

Enhanced Safety and Risk Reduction

Drone inspections eliminate the need for personnel to work at height, in confined spaces, or near hazardous processes. By removing workers from dangerous environments, we significantly reduce exposure to falls, toxic substances, and fire risks. This safety improvement is particularly valuable for offshore operations and facility inspections in live process units.

For pipeline operators, drones reduce the need for ground patrols in remote or environmentally sensitive areas. The aerial perspective also enables early detection of potential threats such as erosion or third-party interference, preventing catastrophic failures and environmental damage. This proactive approach to risk management protects both people and assets.

Cost Efficiency and Operational Continuity

Oil and gas companies achieve 40-60% cost savings compared to traditional inspection methods by eliminating the need for specialized access equipment, production shutdowns, and large field crews. Drones complete inspections in a fraction of the time, reducing labor costs and minimizing operational disruptions. The efficiency gains are especially significant for offshore operations that typically require vessel support.

The ability to conduct inspections without production shutdowns represents a major financial advantage. By avoiding costly process interruptions, companies maintain revenue streams while performing essential asset integrity checks. This operational continuity is particularly valuable in high-value production environments where downtime costs can exceed $1 million per day.

Improved Data Quality and Asset Integrity

Drone inspections capture comprehensive, high-resolution data that provides unprecedented visibility into asset condition. Optical gas imaging reveals fugitive emissions invisible to the naked eye, while thermal imaging identifies process abnormalities and insulation failures. This rich dataset enables evidence-based decisions about maintenance priorities and capital planning.

The digital record created by drone inspections supports predictive maintenance strategies and regulatory compliance. By establishing baseline conditions and tracking changes over time, companies can anticipate failures before they occur. This transformation from reactive to proactive maintenance extends asset life and maximizes return on investment.
